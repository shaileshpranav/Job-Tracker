# Code of Conduct

This is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time. The expectation is simple:
be respectful and assume good faith in issues, PRs, and discussions.

In short:

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- Disagreements about design or scope are fine and expected — keep them focused on the project.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to the maintainer at the email on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/shaileshpranav). Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
comments, commits, issues, and PRs that don't align with this, and may block anyone whose behavior
is harassing, offensive, or otherwise harmful.

This is adapted, informally and at much shorter length, from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
